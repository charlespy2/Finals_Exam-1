Evacuation Guide

Plan where to go and how your household will get there safely.

Before evacuation

Planning an evacuation route

  • Know official evacuation routes and designated centers in your area.
  • Discuss a family communication and meeting plan.
  • Prepare your go-bag in advance.
  • Make arrangements for pets and household members needing assistance.

During evacuation

Community evacuation center

  • Leave promptly when authorities issue an evacuation order.
  • Use the route advised by local authorities.
  • Bring essential supplies and identification.
  • Stay with your household group when possible.
  • Do not return until authorities confirm it is safe.
