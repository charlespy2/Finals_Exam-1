Safety Guides

Learn basic safety actions for common hazards in the Philippines.

Typhoon

  • Monitor official weather bulletins and local announcements.
  • Secure loose outdoor objects when it is safe.
  • Charge phones and prepare emergency lighting.
  • Stay indoors and away from windows during severe weather.
  • Evacuate when instructed by local authorities.

Typhoon preparedness

Flood

  • Move to higher ground when flooding threatens.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Stay away from electrical wires and outlets affected by water.
  • Bring your go-bag if evacuation is needed.
  • Return only when authorities say it is safe.

Flood safety awareness

Earthquake

  • During shaking, Drop, Cover, and Hold On.
  • Stay away from windows and objects that may fall.
  • When shaking stops, check for hazards and move carefully.
  • Expect possible aftershocks.
  • Follow official instructions and avoid damaged structures.

Earthquake safety drill
