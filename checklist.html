Emergency Checklist

Prepare essential items before a disaster happens.

Build an emergency go-bag

Example emergency go-bag

  • Drinking water
  • Ready-to-eat food
  • Flashlight and extra batteries
  • First-aid supplies
  • Personal medicines, if applicable
  • Whistle
  • Power bank and charging cable
  • Extra clothes and a blanket
  • Copies of important documents in a waterproof pouch
  • Cash and emergency contact information

Prepare your household

Family emergency planning

  • Agree on a family meeting place.
  • Know the safest exits from your home.
  • Identify evacuation centers in your area.
  • Keep emergency numbers accessible.
  • Plan how to assist children, older adults, and persons with disabilities.
  • Check supplies regularly and replace expired items.
Keep your go-bag somewhere easy to reach. Adjust its contents to your household's needs and local official guidance.
